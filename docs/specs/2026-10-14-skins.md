# Skins: design (2026-10-14)

The desktop's band, cards, quick settings and setup are drawn pictures (art.ts, kit.tsx). Until now
they had one look, "the deep" (2026-10-12): dark water on either theme, 리플이's glow, ripples,
motes and a wave, drawn controls in glass. Some people want that; others want the band to sit in
the app like one of its own panes, or want an instrument panel. So the look becomes a setting, the
skin, with three values. The deep stays as it is and stays the default.

## The seam

- `Settings.skin: 'deep' | 'flat' | 'matrix'` (default `deep`), in `Frame.skin`. Picked on the
  quick settings panel (a fifth tile) and on the dashboard's Look card. The terminal draws text
  whatever the skin.
- `ui/look.ts`: `lookOf(frame)` gives the skin's `Look`: the props host Text takes for the three
  text roles (ink, sub, mute), a tone's text colour, the Box props of a capsule, a tile and a lit
  control, and the pictures (`Paint`: every drawing kit.tsx and band.tsx take, by name, plus 리플이).
  The deep's pictures are art.ts's as before; flat.ts draws flat.
- **The deep and flat change paint, never geometry.** Every size (`CONTROL_PX`, the switch, the
  tile, the meter, the icons, the close disc, the well) and every cell count (`labelPx`,
  `segmentCells`, a pill's and a word's `cells`) is the same under both, so band.tsx's width
  arithmetic and the blank presses laid over the pictures line up whatever the skin. The matrix
  changes the composition too, and keeps the keys instead (its section).
- A skin that paints no backdrop (flat) returns `''` from `sea`, and the capsule is the Box's own
  `borderStyle` and `backgroundColor`, which the host paints with the theme.
- The matrix is not a `Paint`: it is a composition of its own (`ui/board.tsx`, pictures in
  `ui/cells.ts`), dispatched at the top of `renderBand`, `renderSettings`, `renderAlert` and
  `renderSetup` before any drawn control is built. It shares the host-fact primitives of kit.tsx
  (`press`, `lift`, `pic`, `rowsOf`) and nothing else. Its own section below says what it keeps.

## Flat

The app's own surfaces, in the sense of Fluent: a hairline card, the page's tint, one accent.

- The capsule and the tiles are Boxes edged and tinted in a grey (`rgba(128,124,116, …)`) that sits
  on the light page and the dark one alike; the host paints both with the theme.
- Host Text is the host's own ink: no `color` for values and titles, `dimColor` for names and
  asides. The mod cannot read the theme, and this is the one way the words follow it.
- Pictures paint with theme.ts's palette variables through `svgDoc`, which follow the theme through
  `prefers-color-scheme` as the deep's well already does. 리플이 is drawn in the theme's palette
  (parts.tsx `mascot` has always been).
- The accent (`#6a56d6`, ClaudeRipple's) marks what is on, chosen, or the one thing to press.
  The deep's decorative tones (the judge's teal, the mode's amber, the efforts' depths) are the
  accent here; amber, red and green keep their meanings. No gradient, no glow, no water, no
  weather. Drawn controls have 6 px corners, as the host's own buttons about do.

## Matrix

A board, after EqualizerAPO-XT's Signal Matrix skin (its constitution, `docs/skins/matrix.md`
there): a departure board, a broadcast routing matrix. Not the deep's layout in other colours. The
first version (2026-10-14) kept the deep's rows and tiles, added glows and put a green on
everything; it was a recolour, and was replaced the next day by a composition of its own
(`ui/board.tsx`, its pictures `ui/cells.ts`). The renderers dispatch to it before any drawn
control of kit.tsx is built; `lookOf` never sees it.

What stays from the deep is the function: every press, field and select keeps its key and its
callback (the ui tests hold the two to the same keys). Everything else may differ.

- **Colour is rationed to state.** Green = on, active, saved; amber = bypass, caution; red =
  danger; the accent cyan (`#22d3ee` / `#008eaa`) = engaged, selected, addressed, routed signal
  data; accent2 = the cut side of a bipolar strip. Everything else is monochrome. A cancelled row
  says so by shape (a dashed rule, a hollow amber lamp), not by colour. No glow, no shadow, no
  gradient, no rounded corner, one blink at most (a lamp while something is being decided), in
  `steps()`.
- **Both faces.** The board has a dark face and a light one (the tokens of EqualizerAPO-XT's
  `tokens.json`), switched by `prefers-color-scheme` inside every picture, and host Text on the
  board is the host's own ink: titles in it, quiet lines dimmed, never coloured. 리플이 is drawn
  in board ink through a palette pair of its own (furniture, not signal; its worries keep the
  rationed colours).
- **Every row is a cell with a coordinate.** A 3 px rail and a 5 px lamp at the left in the state's
  colour, a coordinate cell (the kind's letter and the line's number: `E1 AUTO`, `J2 JUDGE`,
  `M3 MODE`, `W4 WARM`, `S5 SKIN`, `C1` for a card), a monochrome mono type code cell, the title
  and its line as host Text in a header band of card ink with the 24 px column grid behind it,
  the controls in a body band of ground ink.
- **Figures are boxed mono readouts**: a sunken cell, a 1px rule, bold mono. The band's facts are
  caption-and-readout pairs; the last route a `> model` board line with its hit in the hit's
  colour; a tip a `> ` board line laid over the facts on hover.
- **Controls are cells.** A power cell (the accent's LED fill with the power glyph while on, an
  outline while off); a port strip for a choice (ports in one sunken strip, a bus lane along the
  bottom, the engaged port a block of the accent with its footprint on the lane and a 1px drop);
  a bipolar LED strip with a centre gap and a boxed readout for the lean; an EXECUTE plate
  (chamfered, a `>` chevron) for the one main action, 1px-rule cells for the quiet ones, a `×`
  cell for the way out. Hover is the crosspoint: the row band and the coordinate column light
  together through one hover scope, the addressed cell brightest.
- **Mono type is measured as mono** (`cells.ts monoPx`: Latin six tenths of an em, East Asian
  wide a full em) and elided rather than squeezed; codes and captions are uppercase where the
  script has case. Host rows are 19 px, so a board row's header band is two of them (38 px) and
  the 24 px pitch lives in the grid texture alone.

## Left as it is

- The deep: moved behind the seam, not changed.
- The terminal: text, no skin.
- The dashboard page itself (packages/ui): it has its own style; only its Look card gains the
  picker.
